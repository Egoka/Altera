## 3. Personal Data Collected

The Operator processes the following personal data:

| Data | Purpose | Retention period |
|---|---|---|
| Email address | Authentication, notifications | Duration of account + archive period |
| Display name and handle | Author profile display | Duration of account + archive period |
| Avatar image | Profile display | Duration of account + archive period |
| IP address and user agent (session) | Security, abuse prevention | Session log retention period |
| `visitorId` (visitor identifier, see [#cookies](#cookies)) | Audience measurement | 2 years |
| Diagnostic IP for engagement events | Reading statistics, bot detection | Engagement event retention period |
| Support request data (request text, email) | Responding to inquiries | Support request retention period |
| Payment identifiers and amounts (when paid services are enabled) | Payment accounting, refunds | As required by accounting legislation |

3.1. The Platform does **not** use third-party trackers, does not engage in behavioral
profiling, and does not share personal data with advertising networks.

3.2. When an article's text is sent to an AI provider for automated content review
(Publication Rules), the text is transmitted **without** the author's name and email address.

## 6. Transfer of Personal Data to Third Parties

6.1. The Operator transfers personal data to third parties only in the following cases:

- **Email provider**: the User's email address for sending system notifications and magic links.
- **AI provider**: article text (without name or email) for content review and AI translation.
- **Payment provider** (when paid services are enabled): data required to process payments;
  payment card data is stored with the payment provider.
- **As required by law**: upon a lawful request (court order, request from authorized authorities).

6.2. The Operator does not sell User personal data to third parties.

## 8. Cookies and Visitor Identifier {#cookies}

8.1. The Platform sets a **`visitorId`** — a random visitor identifier stored as a
first-party cookie for a period of two years.

8.2. This identifier is used solely for measuring the Platform's audience: it allows
distinguishing a returning visit from a new one without identifying the individual. The
identifier is not shared with third parties, is not used to track the User across other
websites, and is not an instrument of targeted advertising.

8.3. In addition to `visitorId`, the Platform uses technical session cookies necessary to
maintain login state. These cookies do not contain personal data beyond the required minimum.

8.4. The form of cookie notice and the need for a consent banner will be confirmed following
legal consultation `[ASSUMPTION — requires legal review]`.

## 9. User Rights

9.1. **Right of access**: obtain information about your processed data by contacting
`/contact?topic=privacy`.

9.2. **Right to data portability**: export your data via `/me/export`. Export is available
at any time.

9.3. **Right to erasure**: delete your account via `/me/delete` (reversible archiving), or
request permanent deletion through support. Upon permanent deletion, financial records are
retained in anonymized form ("tombstone record") as required by law.

9.4. **Right to withdraw consent**: withdraw consent to personal data processing. Withdrawal
means the account can no longer be used; the User may delete their account at the time of
withdrawal.

9.5. **Right to lodge a complaint**: with Roskomnadzor (the Federal Service for Supervision
of Communications, Information Technology and Mass Media).
