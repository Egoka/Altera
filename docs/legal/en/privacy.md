## 3. Processing Principles

3.1. The Operator processes personal data lawfully and fairly, only for the specific purposes set
out in section 4 and only to the extent necessary for those purposes.

3.2. The Operator does not use third-party trackers or analytics services, does not engage in
behavioral profiling and does not share personal data with advertising networks. The Operator does
not sell personal data.

3.3. The Operator does not send advertising or newsletter emails. If such mailings are introduced,
they will be sent only with the User's separate consent.

3.4. The Operator does not make decisions that produce legal effects for the User or otherwise
affect their rights solely on the basis of automated processing of personal data (Article 16 of
the Personal Data Law). A rejection by the automated review of a material is always considered by a
staff member — a reviewer (Publication Rules, section "Review Process").

## 4. Purposes, Data, Legal Bases and Retention Periods

### 4.1. Registration, Login and Account Management

- **Data subjects:** Registered Users.
- **Data:** email address; password as an irreversible hash, if the User has set one; public name,
  profile address (handle), avatar, description and links, if the User has provided them; numbers
  and times of acceptance of revisions of the Terms of Service; account status; bookmarks.
- **Legal basis:** conclusion and performance of the Terms of Service, to which the data subject is
  a party (Article 6(1)(5) of the Personal Data Law).
- **Retention:** for as long as the account exists. Deleting an account in the personal account
  means archiving it: the data are kept until the account is restored or until they are destroyed at
  the User's request (section 9) or by decision of the Operator.

### 4.2. Publication and Review of Materials

- **Data subjects:** Authors; persons mentioned in materials.
- **Data:** the Author's public name, profile address and avatar shown with their materials;
  materials and all their versions; review decisions, their reasons and recommendations; the
  Author's correspondence with the reviewer during review; information about third parties in
  materials, to the extent permitted by the Publication Rules.
- **Legal basis:** performance of the Terms of Service (Article 6(1)(5) of the Personal Data Law);
  for information about third parties in materials — literary, creative and journalistic activity,
  provided that the rights and legitimate interests of those persons are not violated
  (Article 6(1)(8) of the Personal Data Law).
- **Retention:** for as long as the material is stored on the Platform; upon permanent deletion of
  the material or account, the data are destroyed.
- **Publicity:** public profile information and published materials are accessible to an unlimited
  number of persons. The Author decides what to include in the public profile, may use a pseudonym
  instead of their real name, and may change or remove optional information at any time.

### 4.3. Security and Abuse Prevention

- **Data subjects:** all Users.
- **Data:** IP address; browser and device information (user agent); session activity times; records
  of rate limit hits; technical error logs.
- **Legal basis:** performance of the Terms of Service (Article 6(1)(5) of the Personal Data Law);
  for Users without an account — the Operator's legitimate interest in protecting the Platform from
  attacks and abuse (Article 6(1)(7) of the Personal Data Law).
- **Retention:** for the duration of the session and no longer than [TO BE FILLED BY OWNER:
  retention period for session and security logs] after it ends.

### 4.4. Audience Measurement and Material Ratings

- **Data subjects:** all Users who open materials and author profiles.
- **Data:** the visitor identifier `visitorId` (section 8); IP address as a diagnostic and
  protective signal; event type (open, read, bookmark, referral); material or profile; referral
  source; time.
- **Legal basis:** the Operator's legitimate interest in measuring the audience of materials and
  protecting ratings from manipulation (Article 6(1)(7) of the Personal Data Law). The visitor
  identifier is not linked to an account, is not shared with third parties and is not used for
  tracking on other websites.
- **Retention:** the cookie with the identifier is kept in the browser for two years; raw event
  records — no longer than [TO BE FILLED BY OWNER: retention period for raw events], after which only
  aggregate figures for materials and authors remain, which contain no personal data.

### 4.5. Support Requests and Appeals Against Blocking

- **Data subjects:** Users who have sent a request or an appeal.
- **Data:** email address; request text; topic; page address and request code, if the User provided
  them.
- **Legal basis:** for Registered Users — performance of the Terms of Service (Article 6(1)(5) of the
  Personal Data Law); for others — consent, which the User gives by a separate tick box in the
  request form (Article 6(1)(1) of the Personal Data Law).
- **Retention:** until the request is handled and no longer than [TO BE FILLED BY OWNER: retention
  period for requests] after the reply.

### 4.6. Service Notifications

- **Data subjects:** Registered Users.
- **Data:** email address; notification content (one-time login link, confirmation code, decision on
  a material, account information).
- **Legal basis:** performance of the Terms of Service (Article 6(1)(5) of the Personal Data Law).
- **Retention:** within the period stated in clause 4.1.

### 4.7. Compliance with Legal Requirements

The Operator processes personal data where necessary to fulfil obligations imposed on it by law,
including responding to lawful requests from public authorities (Article 6(1)(2) of the Personal
Data Law), for the periods established by the relevant laws.

## 5. Processing Operations and Methods

5.1. The Operator performs the following operations on personal data: collection, recording,
systematization, accumulation, storage, clarification (updating, modification), retrieval, use,
transfer (provision, access), blocking, deletion and destruction.

5.2. Processing is carried out using automated means with data transmission over the Internet.

5.3. Within the Platform, access to personal data is restricted by staff roles: each role sees only
the data needed for its tasks. A reviewer sees the public name and profile address of the Author of
the material under review but not their email address. Every view of personal data through the
administrative interface is recorded in the audit log.

6.1. The Operator transfers personal data only in the following cases:

confidentiality and to take the protective measures provided for by the Personal Data Law.

6.3. The Operator does not transfer personal data across borders.

## 8. Cookies and Visitor Identifier {#cookies}

8.1. The Platform uses the following first-party cookies:

| Cookie | Purpose | Lifetime | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Session tokens: access and refresh | keeping the User logged in | up to 15 minutes and up to 30 days | necessary to perform the Terms of Service |
| `visitorId` | audience measurement and protecting ratings from manipulation | 2 years | the Operator's legitimate interest (clause 4.4) |

8.2. `visitorId` is a random identifier created by the browser on the first visit. It makes it
possible to distinguish a returning visit from a new one without identifying the visitor, is not
linked to an account, is not shared with third parties, and is not used for tracking on other
websites or for advertising. If audience measurement is not enabled on the Platform, the identifier
is not created.

8.3. On the first visit, the Platform shows a cookie notice with a link to this section.

8.4. The User may block or delete cookies in the browser settings. Without the `visitorId` cookie,
visits are not counted in the audience, and reading materials remains available. Without session
tokens, logging in to an account is not possible.

## 9. Rights of the Data Subject

9.1. The User has the right:

- to obtain information about the processing of their personal data (Article 14 of the Personal
  Data Law);
- to demand that their personal data be clarified, blocked or destroyed if the data are incomplete,
  outdated, inaccurate, unlawfully obtained or not necessary for the stated purpose;
- to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, and to demand that processing stop
  (Article 21 of the Personal Data Law);
- to appeal against the Operator's actions or inaction to Roskomnadzor (the Federal Service for
  Supervision of Communications, Information Technology and Mass Media) or in court (Article 17 of
  the Personal Data Law).

9.2. Some actions the User performs themselves in the personal account: editing profile data,
changing the email address with confirmation by code, viewing and ending sessions, deleting
(archiving) the account.

9.3. Requests and demands, including a demand to destroy personal data and permanently delete the
account, are sent via the `/en/contact` form, or to the email or postal address given in section 2.
The Operator responds and fulfils demands within the periods set by Articles 20 and 21 of the
Personal Data Law. To protect the data, the Operator may ask the User to confirm that the request
comes from the account holder.

9.4. When destroying personal data, the Operator retains only the information it is required to
keep by law.
